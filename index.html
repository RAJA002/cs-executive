Companies Act, 2013 — Comprehensive Section Index

Chapter I: Section 1 & 2 — Short Title, Extent & Definitions +
Section 1: Short title, commencement and application of Companies Act 2013.
Section 2(20): Definition of 'Company' incorporated under this Act or previous law.
Section 2(68): Definition of 'Private Company' (restricts transferability, max 200 members).
Section 2(71): Definition of 'Public Company'.
Section 2(62): Definition of 'One Person Company' (OPC).
Chapter II: Sections 3 to 22 — Incorporation & Prospectus +
Section 3: Formation of company for lawful purposes.
Section 4: Memorandum of Association (MOA) clauses (Name, Registered Office, Objects, Liability, Capital).
Section 5: Articles of Association (AOA) regulations for management.
Section 12: Registered Office of Company (Verification within 30 days of incorporation).
Section 13: Alteration of Memorandum of Association.
Chapter IV: Sections 43 to 72 — Share Capital & Debentures +
Section 43: Kinds of Share Capital (Equity shares with voting/DVR rights, Preference shares).
Section 53: Prohibition on issue of shares at a discount.
Section 54: Issue of Sweat Equity Shares.
Section 62: Further issue of share capital (Rights Issue, ESOP, Private Placement).
Section 68: Power of company to buy-back its own securities.
Chapter VII: Sections 96 to 122 — Management & General Meetings +
Section 96: Annual General Meeting (AGM) mandatory holding within 6 months from financial year end.
Section 100: Calling of Extra-Ordinary General Meeting (EGM).
Section 101: Notice of meeting (Clear 21 days notice required).
Section 103: Quorum for meetings (5 members for private; up to 30 for large public companies).
Section 108: Voting through electronic means (e-voting).
Chapter IX: Section 135 — Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) +

Mandatory for companies with Net Worth ≥ ₹500 Cr, Turnover ≥ ₹1000 Cr, or Net Profit ≥ ₹5 Cr.

Section 135(1): Composition of CSR Committee (at least 1 independent director).
Section 135(5): Mandatory spending of 2% of average net profits of preceding 3 financial years.
Chapter XI: Sections 149 to 172 — Appointment & Qualifications of Directors +
Section 149(1): Minimum 3 directors (Public), 2 (Private), 1 (OPC). Maximum 15 directors limit.
Section 149(4): Minimum 1/3rd Independent Directors for listed public companies.
Section 152: Appointment of Directors and DIN mandatory requirement.
Section 164: Disqualifications for appointment of director.
Chapter XII: Sections 173 to 195 — Meetings of Board & Its Powers +
Section 173(1): First Board Meeting within 30 days of incorporation; minimum 4 board meetings per year.
Section 177: Audit Committee constitution.
Section 178: Nomination and Remuneration Committee (NRC) and Stakeholders Relationship Committee.
Section 186: Loans and investments by company limits.
