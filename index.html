Company Law — Sections & Sub-sections Index

Section 135 — Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) +

Every company having net worth of ₹500 crore or more, or turnover of ₹1000 crore or more, or a net profit of ₹5 crore or more during immediately preceding financial year shall constitute a CSR Committee.

Subsection (1): Composition of CSR Committee (Minimum 3 directors, at least 1 independent director).
Subsection (5): Board shall ensure that company spends at least 2% of average net profits made during 3 preceding financial years.
Section 149 — Board of Directors & Independent Directors +

Every company shall have a Board of Directors consisting of individuals as directors.

Subsection (1): Minimum directors: 3 for Public, 2 for Private, 1 for OPC. Maximum limit is 15 directors.
Subsection (4): Every listed public company shall have at least 1/3rd of total number of directors as Independent Directors.
Section 173 — Meetings of Board +

Provisions governing the frequency and conduct of Board Meetings.

Subsection (1): First board meeting within 30 days of incorporation. Minimum 4 meetings per year with max gap of 120 days.
